Overview

Existing robot manipulation datasets are mostly built around parallel-gripper embodiments or grasp-centric dexterous hands, offering limited coverage of tactile-rich dexterous interactions beyond grasping. To train tactile-reactive policies, we collect a large-scale robot play dataset with synchronized tactile observations on our bimanual dexterous embodiment.

The T-Rex mid-training dataset contains 100 hours of teleoperated bimanual dexterous manipulation, spanning more than 270 daily objects and 22 motion primitives. Episodes are designed around object × motion-primitive combinations for compact coverage of contact-rich behaviors, and include randomly selected distractor objects to encourage instruction-following under visual ambiguity.

Data is collected on a bimanual Dexmate Vega-1 robot with a fixed base and two Sharpa Wave dexterous hands.

What's in Each Episode

Multi-View Vision

RGB streams from one head camera and two wrist cameras, time-synchronized with all other modalities.

Robot States & Actions

Per-step robot states and joint-level actions for the bimanual Dexmate Vega-1 arms and two Sharpa Wave dexterous hands.

Per-Fingertip Tactile

Three tactile modalities per fingertip: raw tactile images, deformation maps, and net force/torque signals.

Language Instructions

VLM-generated natural-language instructions paired with every episode, enabling instruction-following under visual ambiguity.

Object × Primitive Coverage

Episodes are structured around object × motion-primitive combinations spanning 270+ daily objects and 22 primitives.

Distractor Objects

Each scene includes randomly selected distractor objects, providing visually ambiguous contexts for grounded policy learning.
